0002Third-person camera, first person when aimingAccepted · Building Play behind Bir's right shoulder. Hold aim to blend into the rifle sights. Main cost: full-body animations that look right from behind.
Phase · Dates

P3 · Proposed and accepted 12 Oct 2026 · Approved by Raj

Who took part

Proposal: game-designer. Reviews: unreal-engineer, art-director, technical-artist, historian. All approve with changes.

The rules
  • Hold aim, 0.20 s blend in, 0.15 s out. Toggle aim for accessibility.
  • Charger reload drops aim. Bolt works in the sights.
  • First aid, ammo, kukri and talking are all third person.
  • No crosshair by default. Optional aim dot in both views.
  • Shots trace from the muzzle, never the camera.
  • Crouched camera clamped to parapet top + 0.10 m. Tested.
Changes from review
  • Separate arms mesh shown only while aiming.
  • One camera, blended in the camera manager.
  • FOV 72° / 65°. Exposure clamped. Bir under 25% of frame.
  • Shared Mannequin skeleton. Carry at dawn becomes a fade.
  • Bir about 35k triangles. Blob shadow on Low.
  • 1937 webbing, kukri at the back, no insignia, headgear swappable.
Resolved disagreements
  • FOV 80° vs 70 to 75°: set 72°.
  • Arms mesh vs full body: arms mesh, the engineer's call.
  • 30k vs 35k triangles: 35k including kit.
Revisit when
  • Testers spot enemies from third person they could not see aiming.
  • The view switch causes discomfort.
  • Animation blocks the first playable more than 2 weeks: fall back to first person.

0001Gameplay logic in C++, tuning as dataAccepted Rules in C++. Everything a designer tunes is data, edited without a compile. Blueprints only wire up meshes, sounds and animation.
Phase · Dates

P2 · Proposed and accepted 12 Oct 2026 · Approved by Raj

Who took part

Proposal: unreal-engineer. Reviews: game-designer, perf-qa. Recorded by docs-editor.

Why
  • Agents cannot read or review Blueprint graphs. C++ is diffable text.
  • Raj still tunes ammo, timers, pressure and difficulty without compiling.
  • Tests first, cheats and a debug overlay from day one.
Tradeoffs accepted
  • No Live Coding on Mac. Logic changes need a rebuild and editor restart.
  • Raj cannot sketch mechanics in Blueprint.
  • Levels, destruction and animation are checked by tests only.
Notes added since
  • 12 Oct: project lives at game/UntilDawn/, module UntilDawn.
  • 12 Oct: Xcode 26.1.1 builds 5.8.3 C++. First build 381 s.
Revisit when
  • A small code change takes over about 2 minutes, edit to editor.
  • Raj often needs to prototype mechanics himself.
